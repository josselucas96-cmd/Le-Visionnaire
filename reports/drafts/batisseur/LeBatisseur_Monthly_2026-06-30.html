Investment Objective

Le Bâtisseur is an unconstrained equity paper portfolio focused on quality compounding and capital allocation discipline. The core targets high-grade businesses, exceptional capital allocators, and category leaders, paired with a tactical layer of thematic plays and select opportunities. The portfolio is designed for long-term capital growth with rigorous risk management.

Market Commentary
  • US equities declined in June. The S&P 500 lost 1.0% and the Nasdaq Composite 2.8%, while the Russell 2000 gained 3.7%. The largest index constituents underperformed the rest of the market.
  • Inflation data re-accelerated during the month and the Federal Reserve's communication became more restrictive, which weighed on long-duration growth stocks.
  • Industrials (+7.2%) and Health Care (+6.5%) were the strongest sectors, Communication Services (-7.9%) and Energy (-5.1%) the weakest.
Management Commentary
  • Le Bâtisseur returned -5.75% in June against -1.03% for the S&P 500, a difference of -4.72 percentage points. The portfolio was fully invested at month-end.
  • Three sets of transactions were made. On 4 June, Ferrari was sold at $349.91 and Broadcom, ServiceNow and Mastercard were opened at $411.73, $121.41 and $477.67 respectively. On 10 June, VICI Properties was sold at $28.45 and CoStar was opened at $34.63. On 12 June, ServiceNow was increased at $98.80.
  • Circle was the largest detractor at 0.87 percentage points. The shares lost about 45% over the month, following bitcoin lower and falling 17% on 30 June after the announcement of Open USD, a fee-free stablecoin backed by more than 140 companies including Visa and Google; the stock was also removed from five Russell indexes. ServiceNow fell about 20% in June, after a 41% rise in May, on concerns about the effect of agentic AI on software spending, margin pressure from the Armis acquisition and delayed contracts in the Middle East. Alibaba cost 0.85 points, Microsoft 0.74, Amazon 0.70 and Meta 0.68.
  • Eli Lilly added 0.47 points and JPMorgan 0.40. The portfolio's beta for the month was 0.38, so the underperformance against the index is attributable to stock selection, in particular the software and internet holdings, rather than to market exposure.
