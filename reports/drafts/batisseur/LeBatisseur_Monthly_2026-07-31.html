Investment Objective

Le Bâtisseur is an unconstrained equity paper portfolio focused on quality compounding and capital allocation discipline. The core targets high-grade businesses, exceptional capital allocators, and category leaders, paired with a tactical layer of thematic plays and select opportunities. The portfolio is designed for long-term capital growth with rigorous risk management.

Market Commentary
  • US indices were mixed in July. The Dow gained 0.32% and the equal-weighted S&P 500 1.1%, while the S&P 500 lost 0.13% and the Nasdaq Composite 3.20%. Capital rotated out of the largest technology names into other parts of the market, and the equal-weighted index moved about 3% ahead of the cap-weighted index year to date.
  • Energy was the strongest sector at about +12.5%, as the ceasefire in Iran collapsed and oil prices rose. Financials gained about 6%. Technology (-3.5%) and Industrials (-3.1%) were the weakest sectors.
  • Second-quarter earnings growth for the S&P 500 was 37.9%, the highest since the third quarter of 2021.
Management Commentary
  • Le Bâtisseur returned +5.88% in July against +0.03% for the S&P 500, a difference of +5.85 percentage points. The portfolio was fully invested at month-end.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • Constellation Software was the main contributor at 1.08 percentage points; consensus estimates for the company's 2026 revenue and earnings were revised upwards during the month. Microsoft added 0.94 points, Alibaba 0.83, Amazon 0.77 and MercadoLibre 0.52. Intuitive Surgical was the largest detractor at 0.31 points and Eli Lilly cost 0.27.
  • The portfolio's beta for the month was 0.21. The outperformance against the index reflects the rotation away from the largest technology names towards companies with more stable cash generation, which is where most of the portfolio is invested.
