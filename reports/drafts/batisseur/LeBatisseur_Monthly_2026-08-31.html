Investment Objective

Le Bâtisseur is an unconstrained equity paper portfolio focused on quality compounding and capital allocation discipline. The core targets high-grade businesses, exceptional capital allocators, and category leaders, paired with a tactical layer of thematic plays and select opportunities. The portfolio is designed for long-term capital growth with rigorous risk management.

Market Commentary
  • US equities rose in August. The S&P 500 gained 2.7% and the Nasdaq 100 4.2%, the latter's best August since 2021. The S&P 500, the Dow, the Russell 2000 and the equal-weighted S&P 500 all reached record highs during the month.
  • Technology gained 6.25% and accounted for more than three-quarters of the S&P 500's return. Energy rose 7.0% and is up 44.2% year to date.
  • Treasury yields rose through the month, with the 10-year above 4.75%, its highest since January 2025, and the 30-year close to 5.30%.
Management Commentary
  • Le Bâtisseur returned +3.49% in August against +2.68% for the S&P 500, a difference of +0.81 percentage points. The portfolio was fully invested at month-end.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • ServiceNow was the main contributor at 0.89 percentage points, recovering part of its June decline. NVIDIA added 0.81 points, Circle 0.57 as bitcoin rose, and Constellation Software 0.44. Amazon was the largest detractor at 0.26 points and Alibaba cost 0.25.
  • The portfolio's beta for the month was 0.83, higher than in July, as the market's rise was led by large technology stocks that the portfolio also holds.
