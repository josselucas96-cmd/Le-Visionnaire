Investment Objective

Le Nakamoto is a digital-asset-treasury (DAT) paper portfolio designed to deliver amplified Bitcoin exposure through actively managed equity positions in companies holding BTC on their balance sheet. The portfolio aims for outperformance of Bitcoin spot over rolling 3- to 5-year horizons through rigorous DAT selection and tactical allocation between BTC DATs and an Income overlay.

Market Commentary
  • Bitcoin rose about 25% in August, including 22% in a single week, to around $77,000. The rise followed the US Treasury's decision to buy back long-dated bonds. Bitcoin fell 3.3% late in the month after Federal Reserve Governor Kevin Warsh's speech at Jackson Hole.
  • Treasury companies rose more than bitcoin. The sector had lost more than $80 billion of market value over the preceding thirteen months, and 43 of the 50 largest companies were trading below the price of their first bitcoin purchase at the start of the month.
Management Commentary
  • Le Nakamoto returned +45.15% in August against +25.05% for Bitcoin, a difference of +20.10 percentage points. The portfolio holds no cash.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • Strive was the main contributor at 18.86 percentage points. The company bought 3,156 bitcoin during August, including 1,800 between 24 and 28 August at an average price of $79,431, bringing its holdings to 23,156 bitcoin and making it the fifth-largest public holder; the shares more than doubled over the month and TD Cowen raised its price target. Metaplanet added 9.45 points, Strategy 8.71 and H100 3.79. All positions ended the month higher.
  • The portfolio's beta to bitcoin was 0.93 for the month. The outperformance against bitcoin came from Strive and, to a lesser extent, from the other companies that continued to add to their holdings during the recovery. Over the four months since inception the portfolio remains below its opening capital, at -4.93%, against -2.04% for bitcoin over the same period.
