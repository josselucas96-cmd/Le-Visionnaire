Investment Objective

Le Bâtisseur is an unconstrained equity paper portfolio focused on quality compounding and capital allocation discipline. The core targets high-grade businesses, exceptional capital allocators, and category leaders, paired with a tactical layer of thematic plays and select opportunities. The portfolio is designed for long-term capital growth with rigorous risk management.

Market Commentary
  • US equities rose strongly in May. The S&P 500 gained 5.3%, its ninth consecutive weekly gain, and the Nasdaq 100 10.6%. The Dow closed above 51,000 for the first time.
  • About 85% of S&P 500 companies beat earnings estimates for the first quarter, against a five-year average of 78%. AI-related revenue was a significant contributor to index-level earnings growth.
  • Following the month's inflation data, markets priced a greater than 50% probability of a Federal Reserve rate increase at the December meeting. The 30-year Treasury yield rose above 5% for the first time since 2007.
Management Commentary
  • Le Bâtisseur was funded on 6 May and returned 0.96% between that date and the end of the month, against 4.52% for the S&P 500 over the same period. Cash at month-end was 5.45%.
  • No transactions were made after the initial investment.
  • The index's rise over the period was concentrated in technology, which gained about 15% as a sector. The portfolio's technology holdings are mainly software companies and rose less, although Constellation Software (+16%), Fair Isaac (+17%) and Microsoft (+9%) all contributed positively.
  • The gap came mainly from the consumer and healthcare holdings. Alibaba fell 12% and MercadoLibre 8%, costing 0.9 points together. Boston Scientific fell 14%, including 10% on 27 May after management lowered its 2026 organic growth guidance, citing competition in electrophysiology and slower Watchman procedure volumes. JPMorgan fell 5%. The cash position of about 5% also held back the portfolio in a rising market.
