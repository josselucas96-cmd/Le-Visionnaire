Investment Objective

Le Bâtisseur is an unconstrained equity paper portfolio focused on quality compounding and capital allocation discipline. The core targets high-grade businesses, exceptional capital allocators, and category leaders, paired with a tactical layer of thematic plays and select opportunities. The portfolio is designed for long-term capital growth with rigorous risk management.

Market Commentary
  • US indices were mixed in July. The Dow gained 0.32% and the equal-weighted S&P 500 1.1%, while the S&P 500 lost 0.13% and the Nasdaq Composite 3.20%. Capital rotated out of technology into other parts of the market, and the equal-weighted index moved about 3% ahead of the cap-weighted index year to date.
  • Energy was the strongest sector at about +12%, as the ceasefire in Iran collapsed and oil prices rose. Financials gained about 6%. Technology fell about 8% and Industrials about 3%.
  • Second-quarter earnings growth for the S&P 500 was 37.9%, the highest since the third quarter of 2021.
Management Commentary
  • Le Bâtisseur returned +5.88% in July against +0.03% for the S&P 500, a difference of +5.85 percentage points. The portfolio was fully invested at month-end.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • The holdings that caused June's underperformance recovered in July. Alibaba rose 27%, including 11% on 8 July on reports that profits had been steady in the June quarter, with further support from a settlement with the US Department of Justice and approval for its Qwen models to power Apple Intelligence in China. Microsoft rose 25%, most of it on 30 July after reporting 43% growth in Azure revenue, the fastest since early 2022. Amazon rose 14%, Constellation Software 14% and MercadoLibre 11%. The consumer discretionary and technology holdings together added about 5.2 points against the index.
  • The technology sector of the index fell about 8% in the month, while the portfolio's technology holdings rose, led by Microsoft and Constellation Software. The portfolio has no energy holdings, the strongest sector of the month. Intuitive Surgical (-11%) and Eli Lilly (-4%) were the main detractors.
