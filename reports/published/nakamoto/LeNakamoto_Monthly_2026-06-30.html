Investment Objective

Le Nakamoto is a digital-asset-treasury (DAT) paper portfolio designed to deliver amplified Bitcoin exposure through actively managed equity positions in companies holding BTC on their balance sheet. The portfolio aims for outperformance of Bitcoin spot over rolling 3- to 5-year horizons through rigorous DAT selection and tactical allocation between BTC DATs and an Income overlay.

Market Commentary
  • Bitcoin fell about 20% in June, from roughly $73,000 to the low $60,000s, with a low for the year near $58,190. US spot ETFs recorded $3.4 billion of outflows in a single week, the largest since their launch in January 2024, followed by thirteen consecutive days of redemptions.
  • The premium of treasury companies to their net asset value disappeared for most of the sector. Strategy's market value fell below the value of its bitcoin holdings for part of the month, and Metaplanet was down 42% year to date and 85% over twelve months.
  • Below a ratio of one, issuing equity to buy bitcoin dilutes existing shareholders rather than increasing bitcoin per share, and the financing model that supported the sector's growth no longer functions.
Management Commentary
  • Le Nakamoto returned -32.23% in June against -20.41% for Bitcoin, a difference of -11.82 percentage points. The portfolio holds no cash.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • The difference with bitcoin came from the three largest positions, which represented about 64% of the portfolio at the start of the month. Strategy fell 45% as its market value moved below the value of its bitcoin, and the company sold a small amount of bitcoin on 1 June to fund dividends on its preferred shares. Strive fell 38% and Metaplanet 32%. Against a 20% decline in bitcoin, these three positions account for about 12 points of relative underperformance, which is the whole of the gap.
  • The other positions fell between 12% and 27%, broadly in line with bitcoin.
