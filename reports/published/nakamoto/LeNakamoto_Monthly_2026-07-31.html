Investment Objective

Le Nakamoto is a digital-asset-treasury (DAT) paper portfolio designed to deliver amplified Bitcoin exposure through actively managed equity positions in companies holding BTC on their balance sheet. The portfolio aims for outperformance of Bitcoin spot over rolling 3- to 5-year horizons through rigorous DAT selection and tactical allocation between BTC DATs and an Income overlay.

Market Commentary
  • Bitcoin recovered 9.8% in July, from below $58,000 to the mid-$60,000s, before giving back part of the gain. ETF outflows stopped during the month.
  • Several treasury companies sold bitcoin to repay debt during the month, and some announced a change of strategy towards AI infrastructure as their share prices declined.
Management Commentary
  • Le Nakamoto returned +6.82% in July against +7.27% for Bitcoin, a difference of -0.45 percentage points. The portfolio holds no cash.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • Metaplanet rose 13%, Capital B 16% and Méliuz 16%, ahead of bitcoin, while Strategy rose in line with it. H100 fell 18% and Strive was nearly flat, which offset these gains and left the portfolio slightly behind bitcoin.
