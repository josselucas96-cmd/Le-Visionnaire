Investment Objective

Le Visionnaire is an unconstrained international equity paper portfolio focused on high-conviction, concentrated positions. It aims for long-term outperformance of the Nasdaq 100 over rolling 5-year horizons, through rigorous selection of companies that compound shareholder value through innovation and structural differentiation.

Market Commentary
  • US equities declined in June. The S&P 500 lost 1.0% and the Nasdaq Composite 2.8%, while the Russell 2000 gained 3.7%. The largest index constituents underperformed the rest of the market.
  • Inflation data re-accelerated during the month and the Federal Reserve's communication became more restrictive. Long-duration growth stocks were the most affected by the change in rate expectations.
  • Industrials (+7.2%) and Health Care (+6.5%) were the strongest sectors, Communication Services (-7.9%) and Energy (-5.1%) the weakest. Bitcoin fell about 20% over the month on record outflows from spot ETFs.
Management Commentary
  • Le Visionnaire returned -2.97% in June against -0.15% for the Nasdaq 100, a difference of -2.82 percentage points. Cash at month-end was 4.54%.
  • Amazon was sold in full on 1 June, with a gain of 11.7% since its purchase at inception, and Zeta Global was opened with about 5% of the portfolio. On 12 June, the positions in Zeta and Metaplanet were increased after a decline in both share prices.
  • The difference with the index came from a small number of holdings. Rocket Lab fell 29% over the month, including about 25% in the first week after the Blue Origin launch failure and as investors repositioned ahead of the SpaceX IPO, which cost 1.5 points against the index. Celsius fell 12%, Metaplanet 28% as bitcoin declined, Zeta 22% from its opening level and Meta 11% with the rest of Communication Services. Together these five positions account for about 5.8 points of relative underperformance.
  • Hims & Hers offset most of it. The shares rose 33% as its direct access to branded weight-loss treatments gained traction, with more than 125,000 Wegovy shipments in the first six weeks, and after JPMorgan published a $32 price target, adding 3.2 points against the index. Nebius rose 20% to a 52-week high and AMD 13%.
