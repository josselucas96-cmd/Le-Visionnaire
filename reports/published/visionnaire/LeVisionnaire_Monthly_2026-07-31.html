Investment Objective

Le Visionnaire is an unconstrained international equity paper portfolio focused on high-conviction, concentrated positions. It aims for long-term outperformance of the Nasdaq 100 over rolling 5-year horizons, through rigorous selection of companies that compound shareholder value through innovation and structural differentiation.

Market Commentary
  • US indices were mixed in July. The Dow gained 0.32% and the equal-weighted S&P 500 1.1%, while the S&P 500 lost 0.13% and the Nasdaq Composite 3.20%. Capital rotated out of technology into other parts of the market.
  • Energy was the strongest sector at about +12%, as the ceasefire in Iran collapsed and oil prices rose. Financials gained about 6%. Technology fell about 8% and Industrials about 3%.
  • Second-quarter earnings growth for the S&P 500 was 37.9%, the highest since the third quarter of 2021. The decline in technology valuations took place against improving reported earnings.
Management Commentary
  • Le Visionnaire returned -9.38% in July against -6.57% for the Nasdaq 100, a difference of -2.81 percentage points. Cash at month-end was 5.01%.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • The portfolio's growth holdings were among the stocks most affected by the rotation. Nebius fell 31% as AI infrastructure stocks were de-rated, with a sell-side initiation on 27 July citing easing GPU capacity constraints and valuation. Tesla fell 26%, including 15% on 22 July after second-quarter results showed operating income down 57%, negative free cash flow and a $25 billion capital expenditure plan. Hims & Hers fell 20%, with declines of 10% on 24 July and 15% on 29 July, when the Federal Trade Commission, Los Angeles County and the State of Utah filed a lawsuit over billing, cancellation and data-sharing practices. Rocket Lab fell 36% and AMD 18%. These five positions account for about 7.4 points of relative underperformance.
  • The index was also supported by large constituents that the portfolio does not hold. Microsoft rose 25% after reporting 43% growth in Azure revenue on 29 July, Amazon 14% and Apple 7%. On the other side, Zeta Global gained 10%, Nu Holdings 7% and Metaplanet 7%, while NVIDIA and Celsius were flat in a month where the index fell, and the portfolio's cash position reduced the gap by about 0.3 points.
