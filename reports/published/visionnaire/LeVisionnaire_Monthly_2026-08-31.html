Investment Objective

Le Visionnaire is an unconstrained international equity paper portfolio focused on high-conviction, concentrated positions. It aims for long-term outperformance of the Nasdaq 100 over rolling 5-year horizons, through rigorous selection of companies that compound shareholder value through innovation and structural differentiation.

Market Commentary
  • US equities rose in August. The S&P 500 gained 2.7% and the Nasdaq 100 4.2%, the latter's best August since 2021. The S&P 500, the Dow, the Russell 2000 and the equal-weighted S&P 500 all reached record highs during the month.
  • Technology gained about 6% and accounted for more than three-quarters of the S&P 500's return. Energy rose about 7% and is up 44% year to date.
  • Treasury yields rose through the month, with the 10-year above 4.75%, its highest since January 2025, and the 30-year close to 5.30%. Bitcoin rose about 25% over the month.
Management Commentary
  • Le Visionnaire returned +11.18% in August against +4.18% for the Nasdaq 100, a difference of +7.00 percentage points. Cash at month-end was 4.50%.
  • No transactions were made during the month.
  • Most of the outperformance came from four holdings. Zeta Global rose 43% after reporting second-quarter revenue of $443 million on 4 August, up 44% year on year, with adjusted EBITDA up 56% and a first GAAP profit, adding 2.4 points against the index. Metaplanet rose 43% and Bitmine 47% as bitcoin recovered, adding 2.4 points together. Tesla rose 18% on regulatory approval for up to 5,000 robotaxis in Las Vegas and ahead of its Cybercab event, and NVIDIA 10%.
  • Some large index constituents that the portfolio does not hold declined during the month, including Amazon, Alphabet and Broadcom, each down about 4 to 5%. Nineteen of the twenty positions ended the month higher, and the largest detractor, NIO, cost 0.1 points.
